# 1009, LTD — Risk Management Policy

## 1. Definitions

1.1. For a detailed list of term definitions used in this document, please refer to the Definitions and Abbreviations Glossary maintained by 1009, LTD.

1.2. If not specified, other terms used in this document have the same meaning as specified in the applicable laws and regulations.

## 2. Context

### Purpose

2.1. The Information and Communication Technology Risk Management Policy ("Policy") sets out how 1009 manages IT risks that the Company is exposed to. IT risks can pose significant adverse prudential risks, potentially compromising the Company's viability. For this reason, IT risk management is fundamental for 1009 to achieve its strategic, operational and reputational objectives.

2.2. The document is prepared under the principle of proportionality, considering the scale and complexity of the Company's operations, the nature of the activity engaged in, the types of services provided, and the corresponding IT risks to which 1009's business processes and underlying IT and information assets are exposed to.

### Scope

2.3. This document describes the IT risk management necessary for the Company to provide its services under Crypto asset service provider (CASP) license.

2.4. Its scope encompasses all aspects of IT and information assets, processes, and third-party dependencies within 1009, including employees, contractors, and any third-party providers.

### Legal and Regulatory Framework

2.5. In the scope of provided services under CASP licence, 1009 shall follow applicable legal and regulatory requirements, leading industry practices and shall ensure compliance of Company's activities with the requirements related to IT risk management.

2.6. The Company regularly reviews identified regulation to identify and keep up to date with any new regulatory changes. Alongside IT risk management framework Company shall minimise the impact of IT risk by deploying appropriate strategies, policies, procedures, IT protocols, tools and any other needed control measures.

## 3. IT Risk Management Framework

3.1. To support the Company's business strategy and objectives 1009 recognises the critical role of maintaining a robust IT risk management framework and ensure business operation continuity and resilience. To foster business continuity and operational resilience within 1009, the Company seeks to integrate its IT risk management framework across all levels of operations, employ necessary competences for IT risk management and promote IT risk management culture.

3.2. 1009's IT risk management framework includes the following topics:

- **Identification and registry**
- Identify and assess the IT risks to which 1009 is exposed.
- Identify and assess whether there are any IT risks resulting from any major change in IT system or IT services, processes or procedures, and/or after any significant operational or security incident.
- Define mitigation and control measures.
- **1009's risk appetite**
- **Classification and assessment**
- **Monitor** the effectiveness of risk management control measures as well as the number of reported incidents affecting the IT-related activities, and take action to correct the measures where necessary.
- **Report** on the IT risks and controls.

3.3. IT Risk Management framework shall cover and be included into 1009's strategies, policies, procedures, IT protocols and tools that are necessary to duly and adequately protect all information and IT assets, including computer software, hardware, servers, as well as to protect all relevant physical components and infrastructures, such as premises, data centres and sensitive designated areas, to ensure that all information and IT assets are adequately protected from risks including damage and unauthorised access or usage.

3.4. To address and manage IT risk, Company shall use and maintain updated IT systems, protocols and tools that are:

- Appropriate to the magnitude of operations supporting the conduct of 1009 activities.
- Reliable.
- Equipped with sufficient capacity to accurately process the data necessary for the performance of activities and the timely provision of services, and to deal with peak orders, message or transaction volumes, as needed, including where new technology is introduced.
- Technologically resilient in order to adequately deal with additional information processing needs as required under stressed market conditions or other adverse situations.

3.5. 1009 utilises a continuous process for IT risk management to ensure continuous improvement. The IT risk management framework is reviewed at least once a year as well as upon the occurrence of major IT-related incidents, conclusions derived from relevant digital operational resilience testing or audit processes.

3.6. It shall be continuously improved on the basis of lessons derived from implementation and monitoring. A report on the review of the IT risk management framework shall be submitted to the Bank of Lithuania upon its request.

### Three Lines of Defence Model

3.7. Following a risk-based approach, IT risks are managed and identified by adopting the three lines of defence model as part of 1009 risk management framework, providing a comprehensive, layered approach to identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks, particularly in the areas of IT and operational resilience.

3.8. To manage IT risks 1009 has built an internal governance and control framework, which is based on three lines of defence model:

- **Defence Line 1**, covers IT systems, processes and IT operations.
- **Defence Line 2**, covers IT risk management.
- **Defence Line 3**, covers IT audit.

3.9. The following responsibilities are assigned to each Defence line:

| Function | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Defence Line 1 | Ensure IT systems, processes and operations; setting, validating, and approving the IT risk tolerance level; ensure set-up of processes and controls; ensure risks are within risk appetite. |
| Defence Line 2 | Manage and oversee IT risks; monitor adherence to the IT risk management framework; ensure IT risks are identified, measured, assessed, managed, monitored and reported. |
| Defence Line 3 | Independently review and provide objective assurance of the compliance of all IT activities and organisational units with the Company's policies and procedures and external requirements. |

### Identification and Registry

3.12. Company shall, on a continuous basis, identify all sources of IT risk, particularly those related to third-party exposures, cyber threats, and IT vulnerabilities relevant to 1009 IT-supported business functions, information assets, and IT assets.

3.13. Based on the identified risks, the company shall define and implement appropriate mitigation and control measures to minimize the exposure. This includes maintaining a Register of classified business processes, IT and information assets, including those at remote sites, network resources, and hardware equipment, and mapping critical assets.

### Tolerance and Acceptance Level

3.14. 1009 shall establish the IT risk tolerance level for IT risk, in accordance with the risk appetite of the Company, with clear roles and responsibilities at each oversight level and shall analyse the impact tolerance for IT disruptions.

3.15. This approved IT risk tolerance serves as a benchmark for assessing and mitigating IT risks, guiding the implementation of controls and treatment measures to maintain IT risk within acceptable level.

3.16. Senior management bears the overall responsibility for setting and approving the digital operational resilience strategy, including the determination of the appropriate risk tolerance level of IT risk of the Company.

### Classification and Assessment

3.17. 1009 assigns accountability and responsibility for the information assets and classifies the identified business functions, supporting processes, third-parties, IT and information assets and their interdependencies in terms of criticality. Criticality is assessed based on the potential financial, operational, and reputational impact.

3.18. Risk assessments identify and evaluate risk, enabling the implementation of control measures to reduce outstanding risks to a level that is both efficient and cost-effective, tailored to the Company's size.

3.19. Company shall perform an IT risk assessment upon each major change in the network and information system infrastructure, in the processes or procedures affecting their IT supported business functions, information assets or IT assets and upon other significant organisational or environmental changes.

3.20. 1009 shall on a regular basis, and at least yearly, conduct a specific IT risk assessment on all legacy IT systems and, in any case before and after connecting technologies, applications or systems.

### Monitoring

3.21. In order to identify any new IT risks 1009 shall ensure the monitoring of:

- Any changes to the IT risk and cyber threat landscape.
- Internal and external vulnerabilities and threats.
- 1009's ability to detect any changes that could impact its overall IT risk profile.

### Communication Channels

3.23. As part of the Policy, 1009 establishes communication channels tailored for both internal staff and external stakeholders. Internal communication distinguishes between staff directly involved in IT Risk Management — particularly those responsible for response and recovery — and those who need periodic updates to remain informed.

3.24. External communication procedures at 1009 are established to ensure transparency, foster trust, and maintain compliance with regulatory requirements.

### Reporting

3.25. As part of the IT risk management framework, Company shall implement communication policies for internal staff and for external stakeholders.

3.26. The results of the IT risk assessment are reported to senior management in a clear and timely manner. The results of the IT risk assessment are also reported to the Bank of Lithuania following relevant regulations.

### Audit

3.27. Independent periodic audit is performed to provide independent assurance of the effectiveness of 1009's governance, systems and processes related to its IT and information security management.

3.28. The audit plan is created prior to an audit and is regularly updated and approved, including IT audits and any material modifications.

3.29. Based on the conclusions from the audit, 1009 shall establish a formal follow-up process, including rules for the timely verification and remediation of critical IT audit findings.

## 4. IT Risk Assessment Methodology

4.1. The IT risk assessment of 1009 is a comprehensive process where relevant threats, including vulnerabilities that may impact the company's business functions, supporting processes, IT and information assets are identified and evaluated.

4.2. The identified threats are assessed based on their probability and impact to the Company. The corresponding control measures are identified, and their design is assessed. Lastly, the residual risk level is determined.

4.3. 1009 categorizes all threats into the following categories:

- Natural and social phenomena.
- Human errors.
- System failures.
- Third-party failures.
- Malicious actions.

### Probability Levels

| Probability | Description |
|---|---|
| Very High | The event is expected to occur in most circumstances. |
| High | Strong possibility the event will occur. |
| Medium | The event might occur at some time. |
| Low | Not expected, but there's a slight possibility. |
| Very Low | Highly unlikely, but may occur in exceptional circumstances. |

### Impact Levels

| Impact | Financial | Operational | Reputational | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Very High | Above 500k EUR | Critical systems unavailable >1 day | Government/regulator inquiry | Disastrous |
| High | 100k–500k EUR | Critical systems unavailable 1 day | Adverse national media | Major |
| Medium | 50k–100k EUR | Unavailable <1 day | Local/regional media | Significant |
| Low | 10k–50k EUR | Unavailable several hours | Local media only | Insignificant |
| Very Low | <10k EUR | Unavailable <1 hour | Insignificant | Day-to-day |

### Inherent Risk Assessment

4.7. Inherent risks are calculated by assessing the outcome of a combination of probability and impact in a 5x5 matrix.

4.8. Combined calculated result allows prioritization:

| Risk | Description | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Very High | Multiple severe/catastrophic adverse effects | Avoid |
| High | Severe/catastrophic adverse effect | Reduce, Transfer, Avoid |
| Medium | Serious adverse effect | Reduce, Transfer |
| Low | Limited adverse effect | Accept |
| Very Low | Insignificant adverse effect | Accept |

### Inherent Risk Mitigation Strategies and Risk Tolerance Level

| Strategy | Description |
|---|---|
| Accept | Accept the risk as the cost of mitigating or eliminating the risk may be higher than its potential impact. |
| Reduce | Reduce the risk by putting in place mitigating actions to reduce the impact or probability of the risk. |
| Transfer | Transfer the risk to a willing third-party to ensure the risk can be better managed this way. |
| Avoid | Avoid the risk by taking necessary steps to completely avoid the probability of the risk. |

### Control Measures

4.10. Subsequently, controls and/or measures that are in place and that mitigate the IT risk are assessed. Control measures are assessed by the level of controls implemented and properly documented.

### Residual Risk

4.11. Control measures that lower the IT risk are matched with the previously derived inherent IT risk to determine the residual risk per threat:

> **Inherent Risk − Implemented Controls/Measures = Residual Risk**

4.12. Employed threat controls either decrease the impact of the threat to the affected asset or the probability of threat occurrence.

4.13. To ensure the effective identification of vulnerabilities to the IT operations, and underlying IT and information assets, 1009 periodically performs gap analysis against information security standards, compliance reviews, internal and external audits.

### Residual Risk Treatment

4.14. 1009 shall establish the IT risk treatment procedure to identify, implement, and document IT risk treatment measures for the IT risks identified and assessed.

4.15. Treatment measures are selected according to Company's risk appetite and residual IT risk classification levels:

- **Very Low Residual Risk** – No tangible measures are needed right now.
- **Low Residual Risk** – No tangible measures are needed right now.
- **Medium Residual Risk** – Case-by-case mitigation; specific policy review and adjustment.
- **High Residual Risk** – Critical, ad-hoc or immediate need for further action.
- **Very High Residual Risk** – Must be addressed immediately, requiring urgent action.

4.16. For residual IT risks that are still present following the implementation of the IT risk treatment measures, 1009 shall:

- Identify remaining residual IT risks.
- Assign roles and responsibilities regarding the acceptance of the residual IT risks that exceed the risk tolerance level.
- Assign roles and responsibilities for the review process of the accepted residual IT risks at least once a year.
- Develop an inventory of the accepted residual IT risks, including a justification for their acceptance.

### IT Assessment Methodology Review

4.18. To ensure the ongoing effectiveness of the IT risk assessment methodology, 1009 conducts regular updates to its assessment process, adapting to evolving regulatory requirements, emerging threats, and internal changes to the existing business processes, control measures, IT and information assets.

4.19. The methodology is reviewed annually or more frequently if significant shifts occur in the IT landscape or the Company's operating environment.

4.20. Additionally, input from audits, as well as insights from previous incident analysis, is used to refine IT risk assessment criteria and thresholds.

4.21. A report on the review of the IT risk assessment methodology shall be submitted to the Bank of Lithuania upon its request.

## 5. General Provisions

5.1. Company, along with designated personnel responsible for overseeing this Policy's implementation, ensures ongoing monitoring to uphold all measures outlined in the IT Risk Management Policy. Regular updates to the Policy or any related process are coordinated with senior management. The Policy is reviewed at least annually to integrate evolving regulatory requirements and industry wide standards.
