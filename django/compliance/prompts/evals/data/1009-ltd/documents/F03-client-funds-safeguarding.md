# 1009, LTD — Client Funds Safeguarding Policy

## 1. Definitions

1.1. For a detailed list of term definitions used in this document, please refer to the Definitions and Abbreviations Glossary maintained by 1009, LTD.

1.2. **"Funds"** means banknotes and coins, scriptural money and electronic money as defined in Directive (EU) 2015/2366 (PSD2) Art. 4(25).

1.3. **"Client funds"** means funds belonging to clients that have been received by the Company in connection with the provision of crypto-asset services, including funds received for the purpose of executing a purchase of crypto-assets on behalf of the client and funds resulting from the sale of crypto-assets pending payout.

## 2. Context

### Purpose

2.1. This Policy implements Article 70 of MiCA, which requires CASPs to make adequate arrangements to safeguard clients' ownership rights over funds, in particular in the event of the Company's insolvency, and to prevent the use of clients' funds for the Company's own account.

### Scope

2.2. This Policy applies whenever the Company holds client funds in connection with any authorised crypto-asset service, including pending settlement of exchange transactions, custody-related fiat balances, and unsettled or in-transit payouts.

### Legal and Regulatory Framework

2.3. Primary anchors:

- MiCA Art. 70 — Safekeeping of clients' funds.
- Directive 2009/110/EC (E-Money Directive 2, "EMD2") — for placement of funds with e-money institutions.
- Directive (EU) 2015/2366 (PSD2) — for safeguarding mechanisms by reference.

## 3. Placement of Client Funds

3.1. The Company shall, **by the end of the business day following the day on which the funds were received**, deposit those funds into one of the following entities, in segregated accounts identified as holding client funds (MiCA Art. 70(1)):

- A credit institution authorised in the Union.
- A central bank.

3.2. The Company shall not deposit client funds with any entity other than those listed in Section 3.1 without the prior consent of the National Competent Authority.

3.3. Where the Company is itself authorised as an electronic money institution or as a credit institution, the safeguarding requirements applicable to that authorisation shall apply in addition to and prevail over the requirements of this Policy where they are more stringent.

## 4. Segregation

4.1. Client funds held under Section 3 shall be:

- Held in accounts whose name and account designation unambiguously identifies the funds as belonging to clients of the Company.
- Operationally segregated from accounts holding the Company's own funds.
- Not commingled at any time with funds belonging to the Company.

4.2. The Company shall not use client funds for its own account under any circumstances. The Company shall not pledge, lend, or otherwise dispose of client funds save on the instruction of the relevant client and only to the extent permitted by MiCA.

## 5. Selection and Monitoring of Credit Institutions

5.1. The selection of a credit institution for the placement of client funds shall be made with all due skill, care and diligence. The Company shall take into account, at minimum:

- The credit standing of the institution (external credit rating, capital adequacy, recent supervisory actions).
- The applicable depositor protection scheme and its limits.
- Diversification of placements to avoid concentration risk above the Company's risk appetite.
- Operational reliability and segregation regime.

5.2. The Company shall review the suitability of each credit institution holding client funds at least annually and upon any event likely to materially affect its credit standing or its segregation arrangements.

5.3. The Company shall maintain a list of approved credit institutions and the rationale for each placement, with allocation limits per institution.

## 6. Reconciliation

6.1. The Company shall reconcile, on each business day:

- The aggregate balance of client funds held in safeguarding accounts at credit institutions.
- The sum of individual client balances recorded in the Company's internal ledger.
- Any in-transit amounts (incoming deposits not yet credited, outgoing payments not yet debited).

6.2. Reconciliation breaks shall be investigated and resolved within one (1) business day. Material or unexplained shortfalls shall trigger the immediate replenishment of the safeguarding account from the Company's own funds and the escalation to the Risk Officer.

6.3. The end-of-day reconciliation is reviewed by an independent control function.

## 7. Records and Client Statements

7.1. The Company maintains internal records sufficient to identify, at any time and without delay, the client to whom each client fund entry corresponds.

7.2. Clients shall be provided with a statement of funds at least quarterly and upon request.

7.3. Records of placements, reconciliations and corrective actions shall be retained for at least five (5) years.

## 8. Insolvency Protection

8.1. The arrangements established under this Policy are designed to ensure that, in the event of the Company's insolvency, client funds are not part of the Company's estate and remain at the disposition of the clients to whom they belong.

8.2. The Company obtains, on at least an annual basis, an independent legal opinion confirming the enforceability of segregation arrangements under the applicable insolvency law.

## 9. Disclosure

9.1. Before providing any service involving the holding of client funds, the Company shall disclose to the client, in a durable medium:

- That the funds will be held at a named third-party credit institution.
- The applicable depositor protection scheme and its limits.
- The fact that the client has no direct recourse against that credit institution save through the Company.

## 10. Review

10.1. This Policy is reviewed at least annually and following any change in the list of approved credit institutions, any material change in the Company's authorisation, or the entry into force of new Level 2 measures under MiCA Art. 70.
