## 2. Context

### Purpose

The purpose of this Human Resource Management Policy ("Policy") is to safeguard the confidentiality, integrity, availability, and authenticity of the Company's information and IT assets. By applying this Policy 1009 aims to strengthen information access control and handling in Company.

The document is prepared under the principle of proportionality, considering the scale and complexity of the Company's operations, the nature of the activity engaged in, the types of services provided, and the corresponding human related risks related to 1009's processes.

### Scope

This Policy covers all aspects of human resource management, including recruitment, onboarding, training, role assignment, and offboarding of personnel involved in IT-related functions.

The Policy extends to all 1009 employees, contractors, and any third-party providers.

## 3. Information Security Responsibilities within the Company

Each employee is responsible for adhering to the information security policies and procedures within 1009, reporting incidents, and safeguarding the Company's assets maintained under their business role. Contractors and third-party providers are required to follow the Company's information security policies and procedures, ensuring that their services and actions meet 1009's information security standards and are regularly monitored for compliance.

To oversee information security responsibilities within 1009 Three Lines of Defence Model is structured and following responsibilities are set:

- **CTO** maintains information security in day-to-day operations, ensuring IT infrastructure, IT assets and processes meet established information security requirements.
- **CISO** is responsible for overseeing and controlling information security, ensuring policies are effectively implemented and monitored.
- **Internal Audit** provides independent assurance by assessing the alignment of implemented information security controls within 1009 against policies and regulatory requirements.
- **CEO** holds the ultimate responsibility for information security within the Company, ensuring alignment with strategic business objectives and regulatory requirements.

For a detailed breakdown of Three Lines of Defence Model and IT security responsibilities, please refer to the Information Security Policy.

## 4. Hiring and Onboarding

The background screening of potential personnel before hire shall be done to reduce internal threats. Screening measures are tailored to business needs, the role, and the classification level of assets to be accessed.

New hires requiring access to non-public information must sign a non-disclosure agreement. Additional measures for new hires include:

- Reviewing, signing, and complying with information security policies.
- Access granted on a "need-to-know" and "need-to-use" basis.
- Regular re-acknowledgment of understanding and acceptance of policies.

Contractors and third-party providers shall be held to the same standards and regulatory requirements as full-time employees.

Upon hiring, HR personnel initiate an access request with CTO. Using a predefined access rights based on the business role, with any subsequent access changes requiring system administrator requests for modification. Each request requires approval from direct or supervising manager.

## 5. Information Security Training and Awareness

CISO is responsible for overseeing the development, delivery, and execution of training and regular awareness sessions for all 1009 employees and contractors. These sessions are conducted during onboarding and periodically throughout employment / contract to reinforce understanding of IT security policies, procedures, protocols and best practice.

Information security trainings include:

- Information security awareness training during onboarding.
- Information security training assignments and phishing simulations.
- Participation in recurring security awareness sessions.

Training for onboarded employees shall be repeated at least annually to ensure knowledge remains up-to-date. Additionally, ad hoc training and awareness sessions shall be conducted promptly in response to unexpected events, significant changes in business operations, or shifts in organizational objectives to maintain compliance, awareness, and alignment with IT security policies within 1009.

## 6. Disciplinary and Grievance

All employees, contractors and third-party providers shall be required to use Company's information and IT assets only for the execution of their business role and accordance to Company's policies. Violations are subject to disciplinary action, including dismissal and potential civil or criminal penalties.

Established disciplinary and grievance procedures address any 1009's policy violations. Non-compliance with Company policies may result in disciplinary actions proportionate to the severity of the violation.

## 7. Reporting and Communication Channels

For routine IT-related issues, general enquiries, or service requests, employees, contractors, and third-party providers are advised to use the IT Service Desk as the primary communication channel.

Additionally, 1009 shall establish whistleblowing channels and encourages all employees, contractors, and third-party providers to utilize whistleblowing channels for reporting any suspected wrongdoings or anomalous behaviour within 1009. Alongside whistleblowing channels employees can report such concerns to their direct manager or to CISO, with assurances of confidentiality and protection from retaliation.

1009 supports transparency by guaranteeing non-discrimination and protection for those who report concerns in good faith, following established procedures. Additionally, regular training is provided to help employees understand the importance of reporting process.

## 8. Employee Contract Termination

Upon termination of an employee's contract, the direct manager shall initiate access revocation to the IT service desk. Access revocation shall be prioritized by system administrator ensuring that all access is revoked no later than the last working day of an employee. Employees whose contracts are terminated shall be required to return IT assets within 24 hours of their final workday, their system access shall be suspended. The direct manager is responsible for monitoring the process of employee access revocation.

For contractors and third-party providers, the contract termination process shall follow similar steps. The direct supervising manager shall initiate access revocation to the IT service desk process and access rights shall be revoked within the last day of the contract, IT assets shall be returned within 24 hours of the contract's end date. The supervising manager or designated point of contact shall ensure that these actions are completed in a timely manner.

When employees or contractors change business roles, access rights shall be adjusted to ensure that only need-to-know access is retained, with excessive rights being removed.

## 9. General Provisions

Company, along with designated personnel responsible for overseeing this Policy's implementation, shall ensure ongoing monitoring to uphold all measures outlined in the Human Resource Policy. Regular updates to the Policy or any related process shall be coordinated with CEO. The Policy shall be reviewed at least annually to integrate evolving regulatory requirements and industry wide standards.
