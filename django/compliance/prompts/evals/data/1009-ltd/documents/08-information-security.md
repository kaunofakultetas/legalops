# 1009, LTD — Information Security Policy

## 1. Definitions

1.1. For a detailed list of term definitions used in this document, please refer to the Definitions and Abbreviations Glossary maintained by 1009, LTD.

1.2. If not specified, other terms used in this document have the same meaning as specified in the applicable laws and regulations.

## 2. Context

### Purpose

2.1. The purpose of this Information Security Policy ("Policy") is to establish a framework protecting 1009's information, as well as that of its employees, customers, contractors, and any third-party providers. By applying this Policy, 1009 aims to classify and protect information with appropriate security measures, ensuring high standards of availability, authenticity, integrity, and confidentiality whether at rest, in use, or in transit.

2.2. The document is prepared under the principle of proportionality, considering the scale and complexity of the Company's operations, the nature of the activity engaged in, the types of services provided, and the corresponding information security risks to 1009's processes and services.

### Scope

2.3. This document describes the Information security measures necessary for the Company to provide its services under Crypto asset service provider (CASP) license.

2.4. Its scope encompasses all aspects of information security within 1009, including all data and corresponding IT assets handling or storing it, as well as employees, contractors, and third-party providers.

### Legal and Regulatory Framework

2.5. In the scope of provided services under CASP licence, 1009 shall follow applicable legal and regulatory requirements, leading industry practices and shall ensure compliance of Company's activities with the requirements related to information security.

2.6. The Company shall regularly review the identified legislation and regulation in order to keep up to date with the changes and identify any new applicable legislation.

### Objectives

2.7. The operational resilience strategy at 1009 shall be developed through comprehensive stakeholder consultation to establish a secure and resilient information management environment aligned with long-term strategic objectives.

2.8. This Policy and its supporting documents (policies and procedures) shall outline the specific information security requirements and guidelines to support 1009's resilience goals and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and best practices.

2.9. 1009 shall ensure that information security, along with associated procedures, protocols, and tools, are fully integrated into Company's IT risk management framework. The collective set of 1009 documents listed in Appendix 1 shall outline the principles of controls to:

- Ensure the security of networks, including the security of the means of transfer of data.
- Contain safeguards against intrusions and data misuse.
- Minimise the risk of corruption or loss of data, unauthorised access and technical flaws that may hinder business activity.
- Preserve the availability, authenticity, integrity, and confidentiality of data, including via the use of cryptographic techniques.
- Ensure that data is protected from risks arising from data management, including poor administration, processing-related risks and human error.
- Guarantee an accurate and prompt data transmission without major disruptions and undue delays.
- Develop and document an information security policy defining rules to protect the availability, authenticity, integrity and confidentiality of data, information assets and IT assets.

2.10. Information security goals and actions shall be communicated to all relevant employees, contractors, and third-parties where applicable. 1009 shall establish a process to monitor and assess the effectiveness of implementation of information security measures, with regular reviews to ensure ongoing relevance and effectiveness in achieving business objectives.

## 3. Governance

3.1. 1009's Senior Management shall provide oversight, sponsorship, and dedicated resources to ensure the effective implementation of the Policy and alignment with operational resilience objectives, regulatory requirements and best practices.

3.2. To maintain accountability and control, 1009's governance structure shall have clearly defined roles, responsibilities, and segregation of duties. This shall include structuring internal functions to prevent of interest, implementing a three lines of defence model, and ensuring that all responsibilities are supported by defined processes and controls.

3.3. Senior Management shall periodically review governance structure, allocating resources and efforts as necessary to meet operational resilience goals and respond to any material changes in information security risks.

### Roles and Responsibilities

3.4. 1009 sets clear roles and responsibilities for all functions and establishes appropriate governance arrangements to ensure effective and timely communication, cooperation and coordination among those functions. This Policy is maintained and updated by the Company's CISO.

3.5. To manage information security risks, CISO's internal governance and control framework is based on three lines of defence model:

- **Defence Line 1**, covers information security systems, processes and IT operations, the responsibility of this line is assigned to CTO.
- **Defence Line 2**, covers information security risk management, the responsibility of this line is assigned to CISO.
- **Defence Line 3**, covers information security audit, the responsibility of this line is assigned to internal audit.

3.6. To prevent unjustified access to information, 1009 follows a principle of "least privilege", where 1009 grants role-based access rights to its employees that are required to execute their duties.

3.7. Principle of "segregation of duties" is applied to prevent the allocation of combinations of access rights that may be used to circumvent controls.

## 4. Information Security

4.1. Information security is the protection of information by upholding confidentiality, integrity, availability, and authenticity:

- **Confidentiality.** Ensuring that information is restricted to only those authorized to access it.
- **Integrity.** Ensuring that all information contained within Company is complete and accurate.
- **Availability.** Ensuring that users can access the information they're authorized to access when they need it.
- **Authenticity.** Ensuring that the source or origin of information is validated through proof of identity.

### Relation with Other Policies

4.2. The Policy is an integral component of 1009's broader IT Risk Management Framework, embedding security practices and protocols that ensure a unified approach to operational resilience, compliance with regulatory requirements and resilience, continuity, and availability of IT assets.

4.3. This Policy aligns with other IT security policies to protect IT and information assets, including critical and important functions by maintaining high standards of data availability, authenticity, integrity, and confidentiality — whether data is at rest, in use, or in transit.

4.4. Below is a brief description of how different type of documents within 1009 together give a substance to the subject of information security:

- **Strategy** defines the overall direction and long-term objectives to support business activities and maintain information security within 1009.
- **Policy** defines the purpose, framework, and rules for information security within the Company.
- **Procedures, process descriptions and work instructions** describe how relevant processes are set up to maintain information security.
- **Annual review** summarizes the information security posture and changes over the past year.

## 5. Information and Data Classification

5.1. Information asset confidentiality classification applies to any form of information, including paper documents and digital data stored on any type of media.

| Confidentiality level | Summary | Examples | Access | Impact if disclosed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Public | Publicly accessible, no risk | Social media feeds, press releases, website content | Anyone | None |
| Internal | Used within Company | Project documents, policies, internal comms | Employees | Low |
| Confidential | May lead to financial loss | Customer information, contracts, partnerships | Need-to-know employees | Medium |

## 6. Access Control

6.1. Employees accessing Company information through systems are assigned access rights based on their business roles, enabling precise addition and removal of roles as needed. Access rights are assigned based on the roles and responsibilities to ensure proper alignment with job functions and responsibilities. All access requests and authorizations are systematically recorded, creating a comprehensive audit trail.

6.2. The business need for access rights is assessed and approved by the direct line manager of the employee, or by the information asset owner. Emergency or privileged access is granted only on a need-to-use basis, with a preference for dedicated accounts where feasible.

6.3. To ensure compliance with the segregation of duties principle, the responsible personnel will prevent the assignment of access rights that may allow any individual to bypass established controls.

## 7. Incident Management

7.1. The incident management framework within 1009 is designed to plan, detect, manage, and notify IT related incidents and anomalous activity in a consistent, coordinated and integrated manner.

7.2. Tools at 1009 shall be in place to identify and generate alerts of anomalous activities and behaviours within business activities, including IT and information assets supporting critical functions.

7.3. Information on all incidents and anomalous activities are recorded, registered, analysed, and evaluated both automatically and manually to facilitate effective incident management.

7.4. CTO shall form the incident response team within 1009, that shall consist of Incident manager and individuals working within 1009 and with expertise in various aspects of IT and information security.

## 8. Business Continuity Management

8.1. A business continuity management process is set in place to maximise the Company's ability to provide services on an ongoing basis, limit losses in the event of a disruption, and ensure the following business priorities are maintained:

- Ensure the continuity of business operations.
- Safeguard Company's assets and maintain operational resilience.
- Minimize financial, operational, and reputational impact.
- Ensure compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements.

## 9. Implementation and Monitoring of Policy

### Training and Awareness

9.1. Company shall develop information security awareness programme and digital operational resilience training as compulsory module in its employee training schemes. Programmes and training shall be applicable to all employees and Senior Management and shall contain a level of complexity commensurate to the remit of their functions. Where appropriate, 1009 shall also include third-party service providers in their relevant training schemes. Training shall be conducted for each newly onboarded personnel and repeated regularly, at least once per year.

### Monitoring

9.2. 1009 shall establish indicators and measures for the monitoring of the implementation and adoption of information security measures across its information assets, ensuring that all required measures are deployed, and relevant staff is trained and informed of their roles and responsibilities.

9.3. Additionally, 1009 shall monitor relevant technological developments on a continuous basis, also with a view to understanding the possible impact of the deployment of such new technologies on information security requirements and digital operational resilience.

### Continuous Improvement and Feedback Loops

9.4. 1009 follows a principle of continuous improvement, incorporating structured feedback loops to adapt to evolving risks and regulatory requirements.

9.5. 1009 shall utilise a plan, do, check, act cycle model for the maintenance and execution of information security to ensure continuous improvement.

9.6. During the **Plan** phase the Policy, objectives, processes and procedures relevant to managing risk and improving information security shall be established.

9.7. During the subsequent **Do** phase the Policy, controls, processes and procedures are implemented and executed.

9.8. During the **Check** phase an assessment and, if applicable, a measurement shall be made of the process performance based on the Policy, objectives, and operational experience.

9.9. During the **Act** phase, corrective and preventive actions shall be taken, based on the results of the internal audit of the information security adoption or any other relevant information.

### Breach and Exception Handling

9.10. Divergence from the requirements outlined in this Policy or its supporting policies within 1009 is categorized as either an exception or a breach, depending on its potential impact to Company's activities:

- **Breach.** A failure to meet defined information security controls, potentially causing operational, reputational, or financial damage.
- **Exception.** A less severe deviation which may occur during the implementation of information security requirements.

9.11. 1009 shall document, assess, and record all deviations. Each exception and breach shall undergo a risk assessment to evaluate their impact on business activities, including operational resilience.

### Consequences of Non-Compliance

9.12. Employees, contractors or third-party providers are required to report any identified information security gaps, nonconformities, improvements or violations of rules included in this Policy or other policies to direct manager, CISO or system administrator via any communication channel (Email, chat, etc).

9.13. 1009 shall establish procedures for disciplinary action and grievances, any violations of information security procedures may be addressed through these procedures. If an individual does not adhere to the information security requirements, the company may take disciplinary action based on the nature and severity of the violation.

### Audit

9.14. As the third line of defence, the internal audit will provide a comprehensive assessment of the efficacy of governance, risk management, information security and other control measures. The internal audits of information security are coordinated by CISO.

9.15. At least once a year information security is subjected to an internal audit. The internal audit will be executed by a qualified internal or an external (third party) auditor of which the impartiality and objectivity shall be guaranteed.

## 10. General Provisions

10.1. Company, along with designated personnel responsible for overseeing this Policy's implementation, ensures ongoing monitoring to uphold all measures outlined in the Information Security Policy. Regular updates to the Policy or any related process are coordinated with CEO and approved by the Management Board. The Policy is reviewed at least annually to integrate evolving regulatory requirements and industry wide standards.
