# 1009, LTD — FinTech Compliance Overview (MiCA & DORA)

## 1. Purpose

1.1. This Overview introduces the FinTech-specific policy set maintained by 1009, LTD ("the Company") to demonstrate compliance with two cornerstone EU legislative acts directly applicable to the Company as a Crypto-Asset Service Provider (CASP):

- **Regulation (EU) 2023/1114** — Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation ("**MiCA**"), applicable to CASPs from 30 December 2024.
- **Regulation (EU) 2022/2554** — Digital Operational Resilience Act ("**DORA**"), applicable from 17 January 2025 to financial entities including CASPs.

1.2. The twelve sector-agnostic IT governance policies (DOC 01–12) are extended by the FinTech series (F01–F12) to cover sector-specific obligations that are not derivable from generic ISO 27002 controls.

## 2. Scope

2.1. The FinTech policy set applies to all crypto-asset services provided by 1009 under its CASP authorisation, including but not limited to:

- Custody and administration of crypto-assets on behalf of clients (MiCA Art. 75).
- Operation of a trading platform for crypto-assets (MiCA Art. 76).
- Exchange of crypto-assets for funds or for other crypto-assets (MiCA Art. 77–78).
- Execution of orders for crypto-assets on behalf of clients (MiCA Art. 79).
- Placing of crypto-assets (MiCA Art. 80).
- Reception and transmission of orders (MiCA Art. 81).
- Providing advice on crypto-assets and portfolio management (MiCA Art. 82–83).
- Providing transfer services for crypto-assets on behalf of clients (MiCA Art. 82a).

2.2. The policies bind all employees, contractors, members of the management body, and third-party providers engaged in delivering or supporting the above services.

## 3. Regulatory Anchors

3.1. The FinTech policy set is anchored in the following primary instruments and their associated Level 2 measures (Regulatory Technical Standards "RTS", Implementing Technical Standards "ITS", and Guidelines):

| Instrument | Subject | Effective |
|---|---|---|
| Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 (MiCA) | CASP authorisation, conduct, custody, market abuse | 30 Dec 2024 |
| Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA) | ICT risk management, incident reporting, resilience testing, third-party oversight | 17 Jan 2025 |
| Regulation (EU) 2023/1113 (Transfer of Funds Regulation, "TFR") | Information accompanying transfers of crypto-assets ("Travel Rule") | 30 Dec 2024 |
| Directive (EU) 2015/849 (AMLD5) and successor AMLR/AMLD6 package | AML/CFT obligations for obliged entities | Ongoing |
| EBA, ESMA, EIOPA Guidelines under MiCA and DORA | Level 3 supervisory expectations | Ongoing |

3.2. National transposition and supervisory expectations of the **Bank of Lithuania** (Lietuvos bankas) as the National Competent Authority (NCA) apply to all policies in this set.

## 7. Review and Governance

7.1. The FinTech policy set is reviewed at least annually and following any of the following triggers:

- Entry into force of new or amended Level 2/Level 3 measures under MiCA or DORA.
- Material change in the Company's authorised crypto-asset services.
- Findings from independent audit, NCA inspection, or material incident.
- Onboarding of a new critical or important ICT third-party provider.

7.2. The Company's Compliance Officer maintains the FinTech policy set and reports changes to the management body. All material amendments require approval by the management body in its supervisory function in accordance with DORA Art. 5(2).
