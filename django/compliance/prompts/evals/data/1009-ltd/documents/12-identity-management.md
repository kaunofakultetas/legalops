# 1009, LTD — Identity Management Policy

## 1. Definitions

1.1. For a detailed list of term definitions used in this document, please refer to the Definitions and Abbreviations Glossary maintained by 1009, LTD.

1.2. If not specified, other terms used in this document have the same meaning as specified in the applicable laws and regulations.

## 2. Context

### Purpose

2.1. The purpose of this Identity Management Policy ("Policy") is to establish measures for the unique identification and authentication of natural persons and systems accessing 1009's assets.

2.2. The document is prepared under the principle of proportionality, considering the scale and complexity of the Company's operations, the nature of the activity engaged in, the types of services provided, and the corresponding IT operations risks related to 1009's processes.

### Scope

2.3. This policy applies to all user identity management and controlling services within 1009, where natural persons and systems access Company assets.

2.4. The Policy extends to all 1009 employees, contractors, and any third-party providers.

### Identity Management Services

2.5. 1009 shall ensure unique identification and authentication of natural persons and systems accessing company information through Identity and Access Management (IAM) and Privileged Access Management (PAM) services. Access rights shall be assigned based on business roles and responsibilities, covering user provisioning, de-provisioning, regular reviews, and continuous logging of identity activities.

2.6. All identity provisioning, amendment and de-provisioning requests must be submitted to the system administrator by HR personnel of an employee or by an employee supervising a third-party. Each request requires approval from the direct or supervising manager.

2.7. These measures align with the access controls outlined in the Access Control Policy. For detailed description on access assignment, monitoring, and revocation, refer to the Access Management and Control Policy.

### Unique Identification

2.8. All natural persons, including employees of the IT third-party service providers and systems accessing 1009's assets shall have a unique, non-transferable identifier assigned. Shared accounts shall only be allowed in exceptional cases with explicit and written CTO and CISO authorization and strict monitoring.

2.9. Comprehensive records of all identities and accounts, including their unique identifiers, authentication methods, and access rights, shall be securely maintained, stored and retained according to the assigned restricted label of information and data classification within 1009. Access to the records is restricted to authorized personnel only.

2.10. Identity and access granted to third-parties, including individual contractors shall meet same or higher information security requirements as the ones outlined for the company's employees, incorporating frequent reviews and adhering to the "need-to-use" principle.

2.11. The control strength of third-party, including individual contractors, access shall be determined by an IT risk assessment.

### Lifecycle Management

2.12. Company shall manage the entire lifecycle of all user and system accounts, encompassing provisioning, modification, review, temporary deactivation, and de-provisioning. Where feasible and appropriate, 1009 deploys automated IAM and PAM solutions and workflow automation tools to enhance efficiency and accuracy in managing identity lifecycle. Stages of account life cycle follows CRUD principle — create, read, update and deleted, which are:

- **Provisioning (Create).** New accounts are created following a formal request from the direct or supervising manager of an employee or third-party.
- **Review (Read).** Regular account reviews are conducted to maintain appropriate permissions.
- **Modification or Temporary Deactivation (Update).** Account access is promptly updated for changes in ownership, role, or responsibilities, with approval from the account owner's direct manager or supervisor. Accounts for users going on extended leave are deactivated to prevent unauthorized access.
- **De-provisioning (Delete).** Upon employment termination or contractual agreement conclusion, all associated accounts are promptly deactivated and removed in line with 1009 Human resource management policy.

2.13. All accounts at 1009 shall undergo periodic reviews to ensure alignment with business role and responsibilities. Accounts accessing only public information are reviewed annually, while accounts accessing non-public information are reviewed every 6 months. An immediate access review shall be conducted if there are any changes in business role and responsibilities and immediate access revocation is done if a contract is terminated or if there is a compliance or security breach within 1009.

2.14. Company shall maintain records of all identity assignments. Those records shall be kept following a reorganisation of the 1009 or after the end of the contractual relationship without prejudice to the retention requirements laid down in applicable European Union and national law.

## 3. General Provisions

3.1. Company, along with designated personnel responsible for overseeing this Policy's implementation, ensures ongoing monitoring to uphold all measures outlined in the Identity Management Policy. Regular updates to the Policy or any related process are coordinated with CISO. The Policy is reviewed at least annually to integrate evolving regulatory requirements and industry wide standards.
