# 1009, LTD — Company Profile

## General Information

1009 LTD is a small and medium-sized enterprise (SME) specializing in IT services and digital infrastructure management. The company focuses on ensuring the smooth operation of IT systems, maintaining high availability, and providing reliable customer service. With a structured approach to operations and technology management, 1009 LTD supports businesses in maintaining efficient and secure digital environments.

## Human Resources

1009 LTD has a team of 40 employees responsible for maintaining system functionality and customer service. The workforce is structured into key divisions:

- **Technology Division (12 employees).** Handles IT infrastructure, software management, and system optimization.
- **Operations Division (15 employees).** Oversees daily business functions, client support, and service continuity.
- **Security Division (5 employees).** Ensures compliance with security policies, data protection, and risk management.
- **Marketing and Communication Division (4 employees).** Manages brand communication, customer engagement, and market positioning.

## Services

1009 LTD provides IT infrastructure management, system monitoring, asset management, and business continuity planning. The company ensures seamless operations through structured IT policies, incident response procedures, and backup solutions. By implementing best practices in system administration and network management, 1009 LTD guarantees reliability and efficiency for its clients.

## Assets

The company maintains a well-structured IT environment, including hardware, software, cloud services, and secure data storage. With a focus on operational stability, backup and recovery strategies, and infrastructure resilience, 1009 LTD ensures uninterrupted service delivery. Physical and environmental security controls are in place to safeguard critical IT assets.

With a dedicated team and a commitment to operational excellence, 1009 LTD remains a trusted partner in IT system management and customer service.
