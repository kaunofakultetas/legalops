# 1009, LTD — Prudential Safeguards & Own Funds Policy

## 1. Definitions

1.1. For a detailed list of term definitions used in this document, please refer to the Definitions and Abbreviations Glossary maintained by 1009, LTD.

1.2. **"Own funds"** has the meaning given in MiCA Art. 3(1)(33), and is composed of Common Equity Tier 1 items as set out in Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 ("CRR") Articles 26 to 30, after the deductions required by CRR Articles 36 to 50.

1.3. **"Fixed overheads"** means the Company's fixed overheads of the preceding year, calculated in accordance with Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/3092 (or the applicable RTS on the methodology for calculating own funds requirements under MiCA Art. 67(8)).

## 2. Context

### Purpose

2.1. This Policy implements Article 67 of MiCA, which requires every CASP to have in place prudential safeguards equal to the higher of (a) a fixed minimum amount per service category set out in Annex IV to MiCA, and (b) one quarter of the fixed overheads of the preceding year, recalculated as necessary.

### Scope

2.2. This Policy applies to the Company's calculation, monitoring, reporting and replenishment of own funds (or, where the Company elects, an equivalent insurance policy or comparable guarantee) at all times during which the Company holds its CASP authorisation.

### Legal and Regulatory Framework

2.3. Primary anchors:

- MiCA Art. 67 — Prudential safeguards.
- MiCA Annex IV — Minimum amounts of own funds for CASPs by class of services.
- Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 (CRR) Articles 26–50 — Composition of own funds (by reference).
- Commission Delegated Regulation specifying the methodology for calculating the fixed overheads requirement.

## 3. Prudential Floor

3.1. The Company classifies its authorised crypto-asset services into the classes set out in MiCA Annex IV and selects the **highest** applicable minimum amount as the Class Floor. The current Class Floor is reviewed whenever the Company extends or restricts its authorisation.

3.2. The Company calculates the **Fixed Overheads Requirement** ("FOR") as one quarter of the fixed overheads of the preceding year, in accordance with the applicable methodology.

3.3. The **Prudential Floor** equals the higher of the Class Floor and the FOR. The Prudential Floor is recalculated:

- Annually upon finalisation of audited financial statements.
- Quarterly on a "best estimate" basis where material changes in cost base have occurred.
- Without delay upon any extension or restriction of the Company's authorisation.

## 4. Composition of Own Funds

4.1. The Company satisfies the Prudential Floor with Common Equity Tier 1 items as set out in CRR Art. 26 to 30, after the deductions required by CRR Art. 36 to 50.

4.2. The Company shall not include in its own funds any item the regulatory eligibility of which is uncertain or contested.

## 5. Alternative — Insurance Policy or Comparable Guarantee

5.1. In the alternative or in combination with own funds, the Company may, in accordance with MiCA Art. 67(5), maintain an insurance policy covering the territories of the Union or a comparable guarantee against liability arising from professional negligence. Any such policy or guarantee shall satisfy, at minimum, the following conditions:

- Initial duration of not less than one year.
- Notice period for cancellation of at least ninety (90) days.
- Insurer or guarantor with an investment-grade external credit rating.
- Coverage of, at minimum, the liabilities arising from a failure to:
- Assess the suitability or appropriateness of a crypto-asset.
- Ensure that systems and security access protocols meet the appropriate standards.
- Compensate clients for loss of crypto-assets or means of access to crypto-assets.
- Compensate clients for damage arising from interruptions of the trading platform.
- Identify and manage conflicts of interest.
- Comply with the rules on safeguarding of clients' funds and crypto-assets.

5.2. Where the Company combines own funds and an insurance policy / guarantee, the combined value shall at all times be no less than the Prudential Floor.

## 6. Liability Coverage for Custody

6.1. The liability owed to clients under MiCA Art. 75(8) for loss of crypto-assets in custody (see **F02 Crypto-Asset Safeguarding & Custody Policy**) shall be covered by:

- Own funds in excess of the Prudential Floor, ringfenced for this purpose; and/or
- An insurance policy meeting the criteria of Section 5.1; and/or
- A custody-specific guarantee meeting equivalent criteria.

6.2. The adequacy of the coverage is reviewed at least annually and following any material change in the value of crypto-assets held in custody.

## 7. Monitoring and Early Warning

7.1. The Risk Officer monitors the Company's own-funds position at minimum monthly and reports to the management body at minimum quarterly. The monitoring framework includes:

- A **Trigger Level** set at 110% of the Prudential Floor — at this level, the Company prepares a capital action plan.
- An **Action Level** set at 105% of the Prudential Floor — at this level, the management body activates the capital action plan and notifies the National Competent Authority on a "no surprises" basis.
- A **Breach** — where own funds fall below the Prudential Floor, the Company shall notify the NCA without delay and execute the capital action plan immediately.

7.2. The Trigger and Action Levels are reviewed at least annually and re-calibrated to reflect business growth, volatility of fixed overheads and risk profile changes.

## 8. Capital Action Plan

8.1. The Capital Action Plan identifies, ranks and time-bounds the measures the Company would take to restore or strengthen own funds, including but not limited to:

- Capital injection by shareholders.
- Retention of earnings (suspension of distributions).
- Cost reduction.
- Reduction of business volumes in higher-capital-consuming services.
- Issuance of qualifying instruments.

8.2. The Plan is tested for feasibility at least annually under stressed conditions.

## 9. Reporting

9.1. The Company submits to the National Competent Authority the periodic and ad-hoc prudential reports required by applicable Level 2/Level 3 measures, in the templates prescribed.

9.2. Internal reporting to the management body in its supervisory function includes, at minimum quarterly:

- Current own-funds position against the Prudential Floor.
- Trend in fixed overheads and impact on FOR.
- Status of Trigger/Action Level monitoring.
- Status of any active capital action plan.

## 10. Record-Keeping

10.1. The Company retains for at least five (5) years:

- Own-funds calculations and supporting reconciliations to the financial statements.
- Class Floor and FOR determinations and recalculations.
- Insurance/guarantee policies and renewal evidence.
- Internal and external reporting on prudential position.
- Capital action plans, tests and any executed actions.

## 11. Review

11.1. This Policy is reviewed at least annually, upon any change to MiCA Annex IV or the FOR methodology, upon any extension or restriction of the Company's authorisation, and upon any breach of the Prudential Floor.
