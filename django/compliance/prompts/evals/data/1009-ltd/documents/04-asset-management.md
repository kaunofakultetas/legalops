# 1009, LTD — Asset Management Policy

For a detailed list of term definitions used in this document, please refer to the Definitions and Abbreviations Glossary maintained by 1009, LTD.

## Context

### Purpose

The purpose of this IT Asset Management Policy ("Policy") is to ensure the effective management of IT assets, safeguarding them against disruptions and vulnerabilities. By applying this Policy, 1009 aims to strengthen operational resilience, and adhere to the industry best practice for IT asset management.

The document is prepared under the principle of proportionality, considering the scale and complexity of the Company's operations, the nature of the activity engaged in, the types of services provided, and the corresponding IT and information security risks to 1009's processes and services.

### Scope

This Policy scope encompasses all aspects of IT asset management, including the identification, registration and monitoring of 1009 owned IT assets.

The Policy extends to all 1009's employees, contractors, and any third-party providers.

## IT Asset Identification and Registration

1009 shall maintain an up-to-date registry of all IT assets (hereinafter – "registry") to support operational resilience, continuity, security, and compliance. Record-keeping shall be supported by IT inventory management solution which shall allow 1009 to enable proper configuration and change management process. The IT inventory management solution shall store the configuration of the IT assets, and the IT asset registry shall contain links and interdependencies between the different IT assets. For each IT asset, the following information is recorded:

- Unique identifier for each IT asset.
- Physical or logical location of the IT asset.
- Classification of IT asset criticality as outlined in IT Risk Management Policy.
- Owner responsible for the IT asset.
- Business functions or services supported by IT asset.
- Business continuity requirements, including recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) specific to IT asset.
- IT asset exposure to external networks (i.e. internet, third-party networks).
- Links and interdependencies among IT assets and business functions supported by IT assets.
- End dates of the IT third-party service provider's regular, extended, and custom support services.
- Any additional information required to perform IT risk assessment on legacy IT assets.

Each IT asset shall be owned at an employee level. Interdependencies between assets are documented to help in the response to security and operational incidents, including cyber-attacks. Threats related to all assets are assessed as part of the risk management process.

IT asset upgrade, replacement, enhancement or end-of-life plan and overall lifecycle shall be employed and reviewed annually by the IT asset owner, and as required in the event of changes. IT asset management procedures are reviewed regularly by CISO.

IT assets within 1009 shall only be used to conduct assigned business roles and responsibilities. For detailed description of IT asset security, please refer to the IT Operations Management Policy.

## Monitoring and Management of IT Assets throughout Asset Life Cycle

To follow 1009's requirements outlined in Company's IT Risk Management Policy, together with IT assets, the Company shall also identify, classify and adequately document all supported business functions, roles and responsibilities and their dependencies with IT assets. The only hardware 1009 uses today is the endpoint devices (laptops, desktops) that employees utilise.

For detailed description of the classification of business functions, supporting processes and information assets in terms of criticality, please refer to the Risk Management Policy.

During the life cycle of identified IT assets, 1009 shall monitor whether IT assets are supported by their external or internal vendors and developers and whether all relevant patches and upgrades are applied, to ensure that IT assets continue to meet and support business operations and risk management requirements. The risks stemming from outdated or unsupported IT assets shall be assessed and mitigated.

For detailed description of IT asset life cycle management, please refer to the IT Operations Management Policy.

## IT Asset Criticality Assessment

The criticality assessment of IT assets within 1009 shall be conducted to evaluate the importance of each IT asset to business functions, with evaluations centred on potential vulnerabilities, interdependencies with other assets, and the likelihood of operational disruptions.

A key component of the criticality assessment shall be analysing the potential financial, operational, reputational and other impact when there is a loss of confidentiality, integrity and availability in these IT assets. Breaches in confidentiality could lead to privacy issues, data leaks, and loss of customer trust, while compromised integrity may disrupt data accuracy and reliability. The availability of IT assets is paramount, as interruptions can disrupt business operations for a prolonged period of time, impacting operational continuity and customer service.

1009 shall conduct an overall IT risk assessment, including criticality assessment on an annual basis or whenever there is a material change to the Company's business practices, operations, or regulatory requirements that require assessment.

For detailed description of the criticality assessment of IT assets, please refer to the IT Risk Management Policy.

## General Provisions

Company, along with designated personnel responsible for overseeing this Policy's implementation, shall ensure ongoing monitoring to uphold all measures outlined in the IT Asset Management Policy. Regular updates to the Policy or any related process shall be coordinated with CISO. The Policy shall be reviewed at least annually to integrate evolving regulatory requirements and industry wide standards.
